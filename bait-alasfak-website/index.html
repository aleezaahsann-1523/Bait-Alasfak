About the company

A Dubai finishing contractor built around detail

BAIT ALASFAK For Plaster Works L.L.C S.O.C is a Dubai-based interior finishing company operating from Port Saeed, Deira, Dubai. We work on the stage of a project that decides how it finally feels — the plaster, ceilings, surfaces, floor and decorative detail that turn a structure into a finished space.

Our licensed activities span the trades a finishing scope actually depends on. That means a project can be handled through one team rather than coordinated across several, with the same standard applied from the first rendered wall to the last sealed joint.

We take on residential and commercial work, from individual rooms through to full interior fit-out packages.

to final handover
